Clinical Data & Economics

Published in AJMC & JPHMP • Harvard/MIT Collaboration

GLP-1 Market Expansion & Efficacy

Working alongside a Harvard undergrad, I synthesized clinical data from over 28 randomized controlled trials (28,000+ patients) to compare the efficacy of semaglutide and tirzepatide (~11.4% mean weight loss). We also modeled the global market trajectory, forecasting growth to $63.54B by 2032, and tracked how adverse events from compounded alternatives drove 2025 FDA enforcement actions.

Published in Oncology Advances

Predicting CNS Tumor Disease Burden

An epidemiological deep dive using GLOBOCAN 2022 datasets to evaluate over 42,000 new CNS cancer cases in the SAARC region. I projected a 22%–70% disease burden surge by 2045 and mapped out neurosurgical density against WHO benchmarks—highlighting that the region is operating at less than 20% of the recommended capacity.
